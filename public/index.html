दिव्यं ददामि ते चक्षुः

“I give you divine eyes.” Bhagavad Gita 11.8

Vyasa gave Sanjaya divine sight, so he could see the whole of Kurukshetra and tell a king who could not see exactly what was happening. Sanjaya does the same for your logs: it sees every line, rebuilds the attack, and tells you the truth about it.

  1. दृष्टि Drishti, to see Tested rules read every line and flag brute force, scans, web exploits, root shells and backdoor accounts. Scores come from code, never from the model.
  2. विचार Vichara, to reason A Gemini agent studies the evidence, joins the attack into one story and maps it to MITRE ATT&CK. Instructions planted in the logs are flagged, never obeyed.
  3. अनुमति Anumati, to approve Fixes come only from a fixed playbook. Nothing runs on its own: you approve each step, and Chitragupta’s ledger records every decision.

For defence only. Demo battles use documentation IP ranges (RFC 5737). Built with Google Gemini.
